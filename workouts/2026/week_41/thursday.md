# Thursday (08/10/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: floor for pistols, wall for handstand push-ups, two dumbbells for the EMOM, one dumbbell for Part B (22.5/15), rope, an 8 m shuttle lane
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; 16' EMOM (4 cycles); 3 sets inside a 9-min cap
- **Stimulus**: single-leg, pressing, and rope skill on the minute, then a short shuttle-press-climb piece
- **Scaling A**: assisted pistols; pike or box HSPU; lighter two-DB devil press; legged rope climbs; devil press 15/10
- **Scaling B**: box pistols or reverse lunges; pike push-ups; burpee + DB snatch; rope pull to stand or 3 strict pull-ups; 15/10 and step-down the shuttle to 12
- **Logistics**: one rope per athlete. If ropes are short, pair the rope and drop that minute to 1 climb so both finish. Mark 8 m lanes before 0:00. EMOM devil-press load is not on the board — both bells together, not alternating. Part B is one bell, alternating, 22.5/15. Board text “1 LL” is a legless rope climb
- **Top Cues**:
  - **Pistol**: “Sit between the heel.”
  - **HSPU**: “Head through, lock out.”
  - **Devil press**: “Chest down, one swing to lockout.”
  - **Rope**: “Squeeze, pull the rope down.”

## Warmup (15 min)

**Flow**

- **Before 0:00**: ropes down; wall lanes marked; DBs and an 8 m shuttle cone line staged, not loaded yet
- **Groups**: lanes of 3-4 that can see one rope and one wall
- **Clock**: running clock; coach calls each block change

- **R1 RAISE (0:00-3:30, easy)**: lines, coach sends waves:
  - 2 lengths easy jog
  - 1 length walking single-leg balance
  - 1 length bear crawl
  - 1 length easy shuttle jog out and back (feel the 8 m)
- **R2 ACTIVATE (3:30-9:00, moderate)**: 3 rounds:
  - 4 assisted pistols each leg (hold a rig or partner)
  - 4 pike push-ups or wall walks to a short hold
  - 4 down-ups
  - 1 easy foot-lock on the rope, no hard climb
- **R3 POTENTIATE (9:00-13:00, hard)**: 2 rounds:
  - 2 pistols each leg (or assisted, faster)
  - 3 pike push-ups
  - 2 light devil-press rehearsals (hands on the floor, jump up, no heavy bell)
  - 1 easy legged climb or a 5s legless attempt, then off the rope
- **Transition (13:00-15:00)**: set the two EMOM bells and the 22.5/15 bell aside for Part B; confirm minute order 1→4

## Post-Warmup Mobility (60-90s)

4. 2-3 **Legless Rope Climbs**

Four cycles. Minute 4 is legless (board: “1 LL”).

**Target**: finish each minute in ~25-40s. Scale on the first missed window, not the third cycle.

## Main Conditioning

### B) 3 Sets for Time

Each set:

- 20 **Shuttle Runs** (1 = 8 m)
- 10 **Alternating Devil Presses** (22.5/15 kg)
- 1 **Legless Rope Climb**

Rest between sets is your choice and stays on the clock.

**Time cap: 9 min (expected: 6-9 min)**

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: assisted pistols, pike HSPU, lighter two-DB devil press, legged climbs; Part B at 15/10
