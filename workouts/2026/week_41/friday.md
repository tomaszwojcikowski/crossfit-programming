# Friday (09/10/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: barbell + plates (deadlift 100/70), pull-up rig, floor space
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; team for-time 24-min cap. Start the piece by ~0:20 so the cap still leaves a cooldown
- **Stimulus**: partner rounds of burpee, deadlift, and muscle-up, then a shared pull-up and deadlift grind
- **Scaling A**: use the board scale on the muscle-up (2 pull-ups + 2 push-ups); deadlift 80/55; step over the bar
- **Scaling B**: ring rows + knee push-ups; deadlift 60/40; 8 rounds; chipper 40 pull-ups / 14 deadlifts / 40 down-ups
- **Logistics**: one bar and one rig lane per team. The bar stays 100/70 for burpees and every deadlift, including the chipper. 12 rounds are I-go-you-go, full round (6 rounds each). The chipper is split, not synchronized
- **Top Cues**:
  - **Burpee over the bar**: “Chest down, two feet over.”
  - **Deadlift**: “Push the floor away.”
  - **Bar muscle-up**: “Hollow, then pull over.”

## Warmup (15 min)

**Flow**

- **Before 0:00**: one empty bar per team; bands on the rig; teams of 2 = workout teams
- **Groups**: warm up in the team lane
- **Clock**: running clock; R2-R3 use full-round tags

- **R1 RAISE (0:00-3:30, easy)**:
  - 0:00-2:00: team jog, side by side
  - 2:00-3:30: easy down-ups in the lane, swap every 20s
- **R2 ACTIVATE (3:30-9:00, moderate)**: I-go-you-go; 3 rounds each:
  - 3 inchworms to a push-up
  - 5 PVC / empty-bar deadlifts
  - 5 scap pulls + 3 kip swings
  - 3 down-ups
- **R3 POTENTIATE (9:00-13:00, hard)**: 2 rounds each:
  - 2 burpees over the empty bar
  - 3 empty-bar deadlifts
  - 2 jumping pull-ups or 1 muscle-up attempt (or 2 push-ups if that is the scale)
  - 3 fast down-ups
- **Transition (13:00-15:00)**: load 100/70 (or scaled). Each athlete states muscle-up or 2 pull-ups + 2 push-ups before round 1

## Post-Warmup Mobility (60-90s)

- 30-45s hip opener each side
- 30-45s wrist and lat stretch

## Movement Prep

1 short team round @ workout load:

- 2 burpees over the bar
- 2 deadlifts
- 1 bar muscle-up or 2 pull-ups + 2 push-ups
- 3 down-ups

## Main Conditioning

### Team of 2 — For Time

**12 rounds, I-go-you-go, full round:**

- 4 **Burpees Over the Bar**
- 3 **Deadlifts** (100/70 kg)
- 2 **Bar Muscle-Ups** or 2 **Pull-Ups** + 2 **Push-Ups**

**Then, split:**

- 60 **Pull-Ups**
- 20 **Deadlifts** (100/70 kg)
- 60 **Down-Ups**
- 20 **Deadlifts** (100/70 kg)
- 60 **Pull-Ups**

Chipper deadlifts stay at 100/70. Split the reps any way you want. Do not synchronize them.

**Time cap: 24 min (expected: 16-23 min)**

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: 2 pull-ups + 2 push-ups, deadlift 80/55, step over the bar
- **Scaling B**: ring rows + knee push-ups, deadlift 60/40, 8 rounds, chipper 40 / 14 / 40 / 14 / 40

## Cooldown & Debrief

- 2:00 easy walk + breathing
- 30-45s pec stretch each side
- Debrief: six full rounds each is plenty. Break the first 60 pull-ups on purpose so the last 60 still move
