# Wednesday (07/10/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: calorie machine, wall ball (10/6 kg), box (60/50 cm), floor space for down-ups
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; team 25' AMRAP. Start the clock by ~0:20 so the AMRAP still leaves a cooldown
- **Stimulus**: partner triplet that changes handoff rules, then a short synchronized sprint, repeated
- **Scaling A**: wall ball 9/6 or 6/4; box 50/40; step-ups; keep the calories
- **Scaling B**: lighter ball; step-ups; 6 cal / 6 wall balls / 4 box jumps; 8 synchronized down-ups
- **Logistics**: one machine, one ball, one box per team. Brief both handoffs before the clock: full round, then by exercise, then synchronized. Second block on the board drops the loads — use the same 10/6 ball and 60/50 box
- **Top Cues**:
  - **Wall ball**: “Hit the target, then sit.”
  - **Box jump**: “Soft land, stand tall.”
  - **Down-up**: “Chest down, stand together.”

## Warmup (15 min)

**Flow**

- **Before 0:00**: machines along the wall; one ball and one box per team lane
- **Groups**: teams of 2 are the workout pairs; warm up in that lane
- **Clock**: running clock; R2-R3 use the same handoffs as the AMRAP

- **R1 RAISE (0:00-3:30, easy)**:
  - 0:00-2:00: team jog, side by side
  - 2:00-3:30: in the lane, easy line hops and down-ups, swap every 20s
- **R2 ACTIVATE (3:30-9:00, moderate)**: I-go-you-go full round; 3 rounds each:
  - 8 air squats
  - 4 box step-ups
  - 4 down-ups
- **R3 POTENTIATE (9:00-13:00, hard)**: by exercise, then one sync set; 2 rounds each:
  - 8 hard machine calories
  - 4 light wall balls (or medball squats)
  - 3 box jumps or fast step-ups
  - then 4 synchronized down-ups together
- **Transition (13:00-15:00)**: set the 10/6 ball and 60/50 box; coach recaps the three handoff rules

## Post-Warmup Mobility (60-90s)

- 30-45s hip opener each side
- 30-45s ankle rocks each side

## Movement Prep

1 tiny team pass:

- 6 hard calories (one athlete)
- 4 wall balls
- 3 box jumps
- 3 synchronized down-ups

## Main Conditioning

### Team of 2 — 25' AMRAP

Repeat this block:

**4 rounds, I-go-you-go, full round** (one athlete finishes cal + wall balls + box jumps, then the partner):

- 10/8 **Cal Machine**
- 8 **Wall Balls** (10/6 kg)
- 6 **Box Jumps** (60/50 cm)

**Then 2 rounds, I-go-you-go, by exercise** (swap after each movement):

- 10 **Cal Machine**
- 8 **Wall Balls** (same 10/6 ball)
- 6 **Box Jumps** (same 60/50 box)

**Then:**

- 12 **Synchronized Down-Ups** (12 each, together — not split)

**Expected rounds: 1-2 (one full pass is about 14-17 min)**

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: lighter ball, lower box or step-ups, keep the calories
- **Scaling B**: 6/6/4 on both triplets; 8 synchronized down-ups

## Cooldown & Debrief

- 2:00 easy walk + breathing
- 30-45s couch stretch each side
- Debrief: the by-exercise rounds are the quick switch. Save a push for the synchronized down-ups
