# Monday (05/10/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: rack + barbell + plates (squat 100/60, then 20/15), jump rope only if choosing double-unders
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; back squat 20 min (every 4:00 × 5); metcon 8-min cap. Start the squat block by ~0:17 so the cap still leaves a cooldown
- **Stimulus**: repeatable sets of 10 with real rest, then a light-bar lunge and squat grind
- **Scaling A**: squat 80/55, or any 10-rep load that stays crisp on set 5; lunges and squats 15/10; step over the bar or 30 single-unders
- **Scaling B**: goblet squat, 5 reps; DB or empty-bar lunges; 15 reps of each metcon movement; step-overs
- **Logistics**: one bar per athlete or per pair; plates to strip 100/60 down to 20/15 stay at the rack; pick jumps or double-unders before the metcon clock
- **Top Cues**:
  - **Back squat**: “Drive the floor away.”
  - **Overhead reverse lunge**: “Arms long, knee down, stand tall.”
  - **Jump over the bar**: “Two feet, clear the bar.”

## Warmup (15 min)

**Flow**

- **Before 0:00**: racks at squat height; empty bars in; a line or empty bar on the floor for jumps
- **Groups**: rack pairs for the strength block
- **Clock**: running clock; coach calls each block change

- **R1 RAISE (0:00-3:30, easy)**: lines of 3-4, coach sends waves:
  - 2 lengths easy jog
  - 1 length high knees + 1 length butt kicks
  - 1 length walking lunge + reach
  - 1 length easy squat to stand
- **R2 ACTIVATE (3:30-9:00, moderate)**: at the rack, no plates; 3 rounds:
  - 8 air squats (3s down)
  - 6 reverse lunges each leg
  - 6 PVC good mornings
  - 6 jumps over a line
- **R3 POTENTIATE (9:00-13:00, hard)**: 3 rounds, faster:
  - 6 fast air squats
  - 4 jumping reverse lunges each leg (or step back)
  - 6 fast line hops
  - 15s easy single-unders only if you already chose the rope
- **Transition (13:00-15:00)**: 5 empty-bar back squats, 4 empty-bar overhead reverse lunges, then load 100/60 (or scaled)

## Post-Warmup Mobility (60-90s)

**Target**: finish the 10 reps in ~45-70s, then rest out the 4:00. Same load on all 5 sets.

2-3 min. Strip the bar to **20/15 kg** and set it on the floor. Confirm jumps over the bar or double-unders.

1 short round @ 20/15:

- 4 overhead reverse lunges
- 4 jumps over the bar (or 10 double-unders)
- 4 back squats

## Main Conditioning

### B) 4 Rounds for Time

- 20 **Alternating Overhead Reverse Lunges** (20/15 kg)
- 20 **Jumps Over the Bar** or 20 **Double-Unders** (pick one and keep it for all 4 rounds)
- 20 **Back Squats** (20/15 kg)

**Time cap: 8 min (expected: 6-8 min)**

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: 15/10 kg; step over the bar, or 30 single-unders
- **Scaling B**: DB lunges + goblet squats, 15 reps each; step-overs

- 30-45s couch stretch each side
