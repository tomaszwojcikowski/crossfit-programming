# Tuesday (06/10/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: one dumbbell (22.5/15) and kettlebells (swings 24/16; a second bell for dual snatches)
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; 12' EMOM; for-time 12-min cap
- **Stimulus**: snatch work inside each minute, then a long snatch-and-swing ladder
- **Scaling A**: DB 15/10; swings 16/12; lighter pair and 8 dual snatches
- **Scaling B**: single-arm DB snatch; lighter swings; EMOM 8 DB snatches / 6 dual snatches; ladder 40-30-15
- **Logistics**: DB and both bells at the station before 0:00. Dual-snatch load is not on the board — set a pair you can finish in ~30-40s. Board text “KB's” is kettlebell swings
- **Top Cues**:
  - **DB snatch**: “Hips snap, lock out.”
  - **Dual KB snatch**: “Punch both bells up.”
  - **KB swing**: “Hips through, arms long.”

## Warmup (15 min)

**Flow**

- **Before 0:00**: one PVC per athlete; DBs and kettlebells staged at the station, not in hand yet
- **Groups**: floor lanes of 3-4; each athlete owns one station for both pieces
- **Clock**: running clock; coach calls each block change

- **R1 RAISE (0:00-3:30, easy)**: lines, coach sends waves:
  - 2 lengths easy jog
  - 1 length skip + arm circles
  - 1 length lateral shuffle each way
  - 1 length easy hip hinge walk
- **R2 ACTIVATE (3:30-9:00, moderate)**: in place with PVC; 3 rounds:
  - 6 good mornings
  - 6 muscle snatches
  - 8 hip snaps (arms long, no bell)
  - 4 down-ups
- **R3 POTENTIATE (9:00-13:00, hard)**: still PVC; 3 rounds:
  - 4 fast muscle snatches
  - 4 snatch balances / punch to lockout
  - 6 fast hip snaps
  - 10s fast feet
- **Transition (13:00-15:00)**: 4 DB snatches and 4 light swings each, then set 22.5/15, the swing bell (24/16), and the dual-snatch pair
