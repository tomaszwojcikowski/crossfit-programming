**Setup**

- **Equipment**: racks + barbell + plates (overhead squat), wall space, pull-up rig
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; OHS 1RM 8 min; interval piece ~12.5 min (E2:30 × 5)
- **Stimulus**: heavy overhead stability first, then repeatable wall-walk / OHS couplet inside fixed windows
- **Scaling A**: OHS to a heavy technical single; metcon OHS 30/20; wall-walk to line or box walk-in
