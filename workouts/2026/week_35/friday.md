# Friday (28/08/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: wall balls 10/6, boxes 60/50, barbell + plates (clean & jerk), team lanes
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; team piece inside a 26-min window
- **Stimulus**: team buy-in, then two I-go-you-go AMRAP blocks with rising clean & jerk demand
- **Scaling A**: wall balls 9/6 or lower target; step-overs; clean & jerks 60/40 then 70/45
- **Scaling B**: med-ball thrusters; low box step-overs; power clean + push press or DB clean & jerk
- **Logistics**: one bar + one box + one wall-ball station per team; explain that both 8-round blocks are full-round I-go-you-go
- **Top Cues**:
  - **Wall ball**: “Drive up, hit the target.”
  - **Box jump over**: “Stay low and keep turning.”
  - **Clean & jerk**: “Fast rack, punch overhead.”

## Warmup (8-10 min)

- **R1 RAISE (2:00 easy)**: 2:00 easy jog or bike
- **R2 ACTIVATE (1:30 moderate)**: 2 rounds:
  - 6 med-ball squats
  - 6 box step-ups
  - 4 empty-bar clean + push press
- **R3 POTENTIATE (1:00 hard)**: 2 rounds:
  - 4 wall balls
  - 2 box jump-overs
  - 2 clean & jerks

## Post-Warmup Mobility (60-90s)

- 30-45s front rack stretch
- 30-45s calf stretch each side

## Movement Prep

Partner rehearsal (keep tiny):

- 6 wall balls each
- 1 short I-go-you-go round of 3 box jump-overs + 1 clean & jerk @ first block load

## Main Conditioning

### Team of 2 — In a 26' Window

**Buy-in:**

- 120 **Wall Ball Shots** (10/6 kg)

Then **AMRAP**

**Block 1 — 8 rounds I-go-you-go, full round each:**

- 3 **Box Jump-Overs** (60/50 cm)
- 3 **Clean & Jerks** (70/45 kg)

Then:

**Block 2 — 8 rounds I-go-you-go, full round each:**

- 8 **Wall Balls**
- 2 **Clean & Jerks** (80/50 kg)

**Target**: finish the buy-in with enough time for both 8-round blocks; fast teams may complete all prescribed rounds inside the window.

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: lower/lighter wall balls, step-overs, clean & jerks 60/40 then 70/45
- **Scaling B**: med-ball thrusters, low box step-overs, power clean + push press or DB clean & jerk

## Cooldown & Debrief

- 2:00 easy walk
- 30-45s pec stretch each side
- Debrief: chip away at the 120 wall balls without redlining so the barbell stays sharp later
