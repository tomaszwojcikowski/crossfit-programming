# Thursday (13/08/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: pull-up rig (pull-ups, HSPU, T2B, BMU), boxes for dips, jump ropes
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; 12' EMOM; 12' AMRAP
- **Stimulus**: gymnastics density EMOM, then an ascending pull/rope/midline AMRAP with BMU skill
- **Scaling A**: banded pull-ups; pike/box HSPU; hanging knee raises; jumping dips; BMU → chest-to-bar or jumping BMU
- **Scaling B**: ring rows; HR push-ups or DB strict press; V-ups; bench dips; pull-ups only (no BMU track)
- **Logistics**: assign rig + box lanes; brief AMRAP progression before the clock; ropes staged at the bay
- **Top Cues**:
  - **Pull-up / BMU**: “Hollow to arch, pull hard.”
  - **HSPU**: “Kick up once, lock every rep.”
  - **T2B**: “Short sets, kick through.”

Quick gymnastics rehearsal:

- 4 pull-ups
- 2-3 HSPU or scale
- 4 T2B
- 4-6 box dips
- 2 BMU attempts or jumping BMU / C2B
- 10 double-under attempts

## Main Conditioning

### A) 12' EMOM

1. 12 **Pull-Ups**
2. 12 **Handstand Push-Ups**
3. 12 **Toes-to-Bar**
4. 12 **Box Dips**

**Target**: finish each minute with ~10-20s rest; scale immediately if you miss the window.

### Reset Window

1-2 min to grab a rope and confirm AMRAP starting numbers.

### B) 12' AMRAP

Start with:

- 2 **Pull-Ups** / 2 **Bar Muscle-Ups**
- 12 **Double-Unders**
- 2 **Toes-to-Bar**
- 2 **Push-Ups**

**Progression each round:**

- Add **+2** to pull-ups, double-unders, T2B, and push-ups
- Add **+1** to bar muscle-ups

Example: round 2 = 4 PU / 3 BMU, 14 DU, 4 T2B, 4 push-ups.

**Expected rounds: 4-7 (keep BMU quality; break early on purpose)**

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: banded pull-ups, pike/box HSPU, hanging knee raises, jumping dips; BMU → C2B or jumping BMU (still +1 each round)
- **Scaling B**: ring rows, HR push-ups, V-ups, bench dips; pull-ups only (drop BMU track); DU → 2× SU

## Cooldown & Debrief

- 2:00 easy walk
- 30-45s forearm/grip shakeout + pec stretch
- Debrief: EMOM quality funds the AMRAP — don’t blow grip on minute 1
