## Main Conditioning

### Team of 2 — 2 Rounds for Time

Each round:

- `1 Lap Run` **together**
- **10 rounds I-go-you-go** (full round each before switch):
  - 6 **Sumo Deadlift High Pulls** (24/16 kg)
  - 8 **KB Swings** (24/16 kg)
  - 10 **Front-Rack Lunges** (24/16 kg)

Then buy-out:

- 100/80 **Cal Machine** (split)
- 50 **Down-Ups** (split)

**Time cap: 32 min (expected: 22-30 min)**

**I-go-you-go standard**: one athlete completes the full 6+8+10 before the partner starts.

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: KB 20/12, Russian swings, keep full-round handoffs
- **Scaling B**: lighter KB/DB, 4/6/8 triplet, 80/60 cals, 40 down-ups
