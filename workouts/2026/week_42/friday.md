# Friday (16/10/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: barbell + plates (40/25), box (60/50), floor for burpees and jumps over the bar
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; team 25' AMRAP. Start the clock by ~0:20 so the AMRAP still leaves a cooldown
- **Stimulus**: shared barbell cycling broken up by burpee box jumps and jumps over the bar
- **Scaling A**: bar 30/20; step-up burpees; step over the bar
- **Scaling B**: dumbbell hang snatch and dumbbell overhead squat; 20 / 20 / 20 / 40 reps; step-overs
- **Logistics**: one bar and one box per team. Split each movement, then start the next line together. Same 40/25 bar for the hang squat snatch and the overhead squat. Bar on the floor for the jumps. Step down off the box
- **Top Cues**:
  - **Hang squat snatch**: “Hips back, catch deep.”
  - **Burpee box jump**: “Chest down, two feet up.”
  - **Jump over the bar**: “Two feet, clear the bar.”

## Warmup (15 min)

**Flow**

- **Before 0:00**: one empty bar and one box per team; teams of 2 are the workout teams
- **Groups**: warm up in the team lane
- **Clock**: running clock; R2-R3 swap each round

- **R1 RAISE (0:00-3:30, easy)**:
  - 0:00-2:00: team jog, side by side
  - 2:00-3:30: easy line hops and down-ups, swap every 20s
- **R2 ACTIVATE (3:30-9:00, moderate)**: PVC or empty bar; 3 rounds each:
  - 5 muscle snatches
  - 5 overhead squats
  - 4 reverse lunges each leg
  - 3 down-ups
- **R3 POTENTIATE (9:00-13:00, hard)**: 2 rounds each:
  - 2 hang power snatches
  - 2 overhead squats
  - 2 burpees to a step-up
  - 3 jumps over a line
- **Transition (13:00-15:00)**: 2 empty-bar hang squat snatches and 2 overhead squats. Load 40/25 only after both partners have a scale

## Post-Warmup Mobility (60-90s)

- 30-45s lat stretch each side
- 30-45s ankle rocks each side

## Movement Prep

1 short team pass @ 40/25 (or scaled):

- 2 hang squat snatches
- 2 burpee box jumps or step-ups
- 2 overhead squats
- 4 jumps over the bar

## Main Conditioning

### Team of 2 — 25' AMRAP

Split each line. Move to the next line together.

- 30 **Hang Squat Snatches** (40/25 kg)
- 30 **Burpee Box Jumps** (60/50 cm)
- 30 **Overhead Squats** (40/25 kg)
- 60 **Jumps Over the Bar**

One shared score. Step down off the box. The bar stays 40/25 for both the snatch and the overhead squat.

**Expected rounds: 2-3 (one split round is about 8-12 min)**

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: 30/20 kg; step-up burpees; step over the bar
- **Scaling B**: dumbbell hang snatch and overhead squat; 20 / 20 / 20 / 40; step-overs

## Cooldown & Debrief

- 2:00 easy walk + breathing
- 30-45s lat and hip stretch
- Debrief: split the 30s before you are tired. The jumps are where teams stall
