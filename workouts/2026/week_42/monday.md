# Monday (12/10/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: rack + barbell + plates (front squat @ 80% 1RM, then 60/35), calorie machine, floor for down-ups
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; front squat every 2:00 × 5 (10 min); then every 2:00 × 10 alternating (20 min). Start the squats by ~0:17 so the 20-minute piece still leaves a cooldown
- **Stimulus**: heavy sets of 5 with short rest, then fast barbell cycling traded with an easy engine minute
- **Scaling A**: a 5-rep squat with 3 reps left, or ~70%; bar 50/30; same reps
- **Scaling B**: goblet squat, 5 reps; DB clean + front squat + push press, 4 reps; 8 cal + 5 down-ups
- **Logistics**: one bar and one machine per athlete. No 1RM on the board — use a known 80%, or a 5-rep load you could do for 7, and keep it for all 5 sets. Odd minutes are the barbell. Even minutes are the machine. STO is shoulder-to-overhead on the same 60/35 bar
- **Top Cues**:
  - **Front squat**: “Drive the floor away.”
  - **Power clean**: “Jump, then punch the elbows.”
  - **STO**: “Dip straight, punch up.”

## Warmup (15 min)

**Flow**

- **Before 0:00**: racks at front-squat height; one PVC and one empty bar per athlete; machines free for the even minutes
- **Groups**: one athlete per rack
- **Clock**: running clock; coach calls each block change

- **R1 RAISE (0:00-3:30, easy)**: lines of 3-4, coach sends waves:
  - 2 lengths easy jog
  - 1 length high knees + 1 length butt kicks
  - 1 length walking lunge + reach
  - 1 length easy squat to stand
- **R2 ACTIVATE (3:30-9:00, moderate)**: at the rack with PVC, no plates; 3 rounds:
  - 6 squats (elbows up, 3s down)
  - 5 muscle cleans
  - 5 front squats
  - 4 down-ups
- **R3 POTENTIATE (9:00-13:00, hard)**: still PVC; 3 rounds, faster:
  - 3 fast muscle cleans
  - 3 front squats
  - 3 push presses
  - 4 fast down-ups
- **Transition (13:00-15:00)**: 3 empty-bar power cleans, 3 front squats, 2 push presses. Leave the 80% plates at the rack

## Post-Warmup Mobility (60-90s)

- 30-45s front-rack stretch each side
- 30-45s ankle rocks each side

## Strength / Skill

### A) Front Squat — Every 2:00 × 5 Sets

- 5 reps @ **80% 1RM**

Board text is “5x5”. That is 5 reps for 5 sets.

**Target**: finish the 5 in ~20-35s, then rest out the 2:00. Same load on all 5 sets. If set 1 grinds, drop the load before set 2.

## Reset Window

2 min. Change the bar to **60/35 kg**. Stand next to the calorie machine.

## Movement Prep

1 short round @ 60/35:

- 2 power cleans
- 2 front squats
- 2 shoulder-to-overhead
- 6 calories
- 3 down-ups

## Main Conditioning

### B) Every 2:00 × 10 Sets — Alternate a / b

**Odd sets (1, 3, 5, 7, 9):**

- 6 **Power Cleans** (60/35 kg)
- 6 **Front Squats**
- 6 **Shoulder-to-Overhead**

**Even sets (2, 4, 6, 8, 10):**

- 12 **Cal Machine**
- 8 **Down-Ups**

**Target**: barbell set in ~45-70s, machine set in ~40-60s, then rest out the 2:00. One bar for the clean, the front squat, and the shoulder-to-overhead.

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: 50/30 kg, same reps
- **Scaling B**: 4 DB cleans + 4 front squats + 4 push presses; 8 cal + 5 down-ups

## Cooldown & Debrief

- 1:00 easy walk + breathing
- 30-45s front-rack stretch
- Debrief: use the full 2:00 on the heavy sets. On the metcon, finish the barbell and wait
