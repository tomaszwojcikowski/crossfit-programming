# Tuesday (13/10/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: kettlebell (24/16), box (60/50), wall ball (10/6), jump rope, wall for handstand push-ups
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; swing test 7-min cap; 7' AMRAP, 2' rest, 7' AMRAP. Start the test by ~0:18
- **Stimulus**: kettlebell swings with a push-up tax on every break, then two short mixed AMRAPs
- **Scaling A**: bell 16/12; box 50/40 or step-ups; pike handstand push-ups; wall ball 6/4; 45 single-unders; lighter one-arm press
- **Scaling B**: 70 swings and 3 push-ups per break; 8 step-ups / 6 pike push-ups / 8 light wall balls; 20 swings / 40 single-unders / 6 one-arm push presses
- **Logistics**: one bell, one box, one ball, one rope per athlete. A break is letting go of the bell — 5 push-ups, then pick it up. The one-arm press uses that same 24/16 bell. Switch arms each new round, right then left
- **Top Cues**:
  - **KB swing**: “Hips through, arms long.”
  - **HSPU**: “Head through, lock out.”
  - **One-arm press**: “Punch the bell up.”

## Warmup (15 min)

**Flow**

- **Before 0:00**: bell, box, ball, and rope staged at the station; wall lane marked. Nothing in hand yet
- **Groups**: one athlete per station for all three pieces
- **Clock**: running clock; coach calls each block change

- **R1 RAISE (0:00-3:30, easy)**: lines, coach sends waves:
  - 2 lengths easy jog
  - 1 length skip + arm circles
  - 1 length lateral shuffle each way
  - 1 length easy hip-hinge walk
- **R2 ACTIVATE (3:30-9:00, moderate)**: in place, no bell; 3 rounds:
  - 8 hip snaps (arms long)
  - 5 push-ups
  - 5 air squats
  - 4 down-ups
- **R3 POTENTIATE (9:00-13:00, hard)**: 2 rounds:
  - 8 fast hip snaps
  - 4 push-ups
  - 3 pike push-ups
  - 15s easy single-unders
- **Transition (13:00-15:00)**: 8 light swings and 3 push-ups, then set the 24/16 bell

## Post-Warmup Mobility (60-90s)

- 30-45s hip flexor stretch each side
- 30-45s wrist extension stretch

## Movement Prep

1 short swing rehearsal:

- 10 kettlebell swings (light, or the 24/16 if it is already easy)
- 3 push-ups

## Main Conditioning

### A) 100 Kettlebell Swings — For Time

- 100 **Kettlebell Swings** (24/16 kg)
- Every break: 5 **Push-Ups**, then back to the bell

**Time cap: 7 min (expected: 4-7 min)**

A break is the bell leaving your hands. Sets of 15-25 keep the push-up tax small.

### Reset (2 min)

Shake out the grip. Set the 60/50 box and the 10/6 ball. Name the handstand push-up scale before the clock: full, pike, or box. Touch 3 box jumps, 2 pike push-ups, 4 wall balls, and 10 single-unders.

### B1) 7' AMRAP

- 10 **Box Jumps** (60/50 cm)
- 10 **Handstand Push-Ups**
- 10 **Wall Balls** (10/6 kg)

**Expected rounds: 2-4 (one round is about 2:00-3:00; HSPU is the limiter)**

Step down off the box.

### Rest 2:00

On the board. Rope in hand. Press the bell once each arm so round 1 already has a side.

### B2) 7' AMRAP

- 30 **Kettlebell Swings** (24/16 kg)
- 30 **Double-Unders**
- 10 **One-Arm Kettlebell Presses** (same 24/16 bell)

Switch arms each new round. Round 1 right, round 2 left.

**Expected rounds: 2-4 (one round is about 2:00-3:00)**

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: bell 16/12; lower box or step-ups; pike HSPU; lighter ball; 45 single-unders; lighter press
- **Scaling B**: 70 swings and 3 push-ups per break; first AMRAP 8 / 6 / 8; second AMRAP 20 swings, 40 single-unders, 6 one-arm push presses

## Cooldown & Debrief

- 1:30 easy walk + breathing
- 30-45s forearm shakeout
- Debrief: the push-ups are the tax for putting the bell down. Small sets still win
