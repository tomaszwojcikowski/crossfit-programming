# Wednesday (14/10/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: pull-up rig, barbell + plates (40/25), floor for down-ups
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; team for-time 28-min cap. Start the piece by ~0:19 so the cap still leaves a cooldown
- **Stimulus**: shared gymnastics on both ends, with a barbell ladder and synchronized down-ups in the middle
- **Scaling A**: snatch and lunge 30/20; banded pull-ups; knees-to-elbows for some of the toes-to-bar
- **Scaling B**: 40 pull-ups and 40 toes-to-bar on each gymnastics block; snatch 25/15 or a dumbbell; empty-bar lunges; down-ups 21-15-9
- **Logistics**: one bar and one rig lane per team. Pull-ups, toes-to-bar, power snatches, and lunges are split. Down-ups are the only synchronized piece. Get the bar to the back however you like — only the lunge steps count. Each step is 1 rep
- **Top Cues**:
  - **Pull-up**: “Chin over the bar.”
  - **Power snatch**: “Pull under, receive high.”
  - **Down-up**: “Chest down, stand together.”

## Warmup (15 min)

**Flow**

- **Before 0:00**: one empty bar and one PVC per team; bands on the rig; teams of 2 are the workout teams
- **Groups**: warm up in the team lane
- **Clock**: running clock; coach calls each block change

- **R1 RAISE (0:00-3:30, easy)**:
  - 0:00-2:00: team jog, side by side
  - 2:00-3:30: easy down-ups in the lane, swap every 20s
- **R2 ACTIVATE (3:30-9:00, moderate)**: PVC, I-go-you-go; 3 rounds each:
  - 5 scap pulls + 3 kip swings
  - 4 muscle snatches
  - 4 reverse lunges each leg
  - 4 down-ups
- **R3 POTENTIATE (9:00-13:00, hard)**: 2 rounds each:
  - 3 jumping pull-ups or 3 kip swings
  - 3 fast muscle snatches
  - 3 hanging knee raises
  - 3 fast down-ups
- **Transition (13:00-15:00)**: 2 empty-bar power snatches, 4 reverse lunges, 1 easy set of toes-to-bar or knee raises. Coach recaps split vs synchronized before anyone loads 40/25

## Post-Warmup Mobility (60-90s)

- 30-45s lat stretch each side
- 30-45s hip opener each side

## Movement Prep

1 short team pass @ 40/25 (or scaled):

- 3 pull-ups
- 3 toes-to-bar or knee raises
- 2 power snatches
- 4 back-rack lunges
- 3 synchronized down-ups

## Main Conditioning

### Team of 2 — For Time

**Split:**

- 66 **Pull-Ups**
- 66 **Toes-to-Bar**

**Then 33-22-11 of each**, snatches, then lunges, then down-ups:

- **Power Snatches** (40/25 kg) — split
- **Back-Rack Lunges** (40/25 kg) — split, each step is 1
- **Synchronized Down-Ups** — both do every rep, together

**Then, split:**

- 66 **Toes-to-Bar**
- 66 **Pull-Ups**

A synchronized rep counts when you stand up together. Split the other reps any way you want.

**Time cap: 28 min (expected: 22-28 min)**

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: 30/20 kg; banded pull-ups; mix in knees-to-elbows
- **Scaling B**: 40 / 40 on each gymnastics block; lighter snatch; empty-bar lunges; down-ups 21-15-9

## Cooldown & Debrief

- 2:00 easy walk + breathing
- 30-45s lat and hip stretch
- Debrief: break the first 66 on purpose. Cycle the snatches in chunks
