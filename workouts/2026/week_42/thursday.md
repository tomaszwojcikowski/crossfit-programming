# Thursday (15/10/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: wall for wall walks, a marked 350 m loop, calorie machine, open floor for 10 m handstand walk or 30 m bear walk
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; 6 sets of 4:00 AMRAP + 1:00 rest (30 min). Start set 1 by ~0:18
- **Stimulus**: invert and run, then machine and handstand walk, with a full minute off between sets
- **Scaling A**: 3 wall walks so a lap still happens; bear walk if the handstand walk breaks; same calories
- **Scaling B**: shorter wall walk, feet to a line; 1 lap or 200 m; 8 cal + 20 m bear crawl
- **Logistics**: one machine per athlete on the even sets (row, bike, or ski). Odd sets are the loop — mark 350 m before 0:00. Handstand walk and bear walk are the board’s pair: do 10 m handstand walk, or 30 m bear walk, and keep that choice for all three even sets. 1 lap = 350 m
- **Top Cues**:
  - **Wall walk**: “Walk the feet in, then walk them out.”
  - **Handstand walk**: “Kick, then step.”
  - **Bear walk**: “Knees low, keep moving.”

## Warmup (15 min)

**Flow**

- **Before 0:00**: wall lanes marked; 350 m loop coned; machines staged along the wall
- **Groups**: lanes of 3-4 that can see one wall. Everyone uses the same loop
- **Clock**: running clock; coach calls each block change

- **R1 RAISE (0:00-3:30, easy)**: lines, coach sends waves:
  - 2 lengths easy jog
  - 1 length high knees + 1 length butt kicks
  - 1 length bear crawl
  - 1 length easy jog
- **R2 ACTIVATE (3:30-9:00, moderate)**: at the wall; 3 rounds:
  - 1 wall walk, or walk to a line and back
  - 4 down-ups
  - 8 m bear walk
  - 4 inchworms
- **R3 POTENTIATE (9:00-13:00, hard)**: 2 rounds:
  - 1 faster wall walk
  - 5 hard calories
  - 5 m handstand walk or 15 m fast bear walk
  - 10s fast feet
- **Transition (13:00-15:00)**: walk the first corner of the loop so the turn is obvious. State handstand walk or bear walk before set 1

## Post-Warmup Mobility (60-90s)

- 30-45s wrist extension stretch
- 30-45s lat stretch each side

## Movement Prep

1 tiny pass:

- 1 short wall walk
- 5 calories
- 5 m bear walk

## Main Conditioning

### Every 4:00 Work + 1:00 Rest × 6 Sets — Alternate a / b

**Odd sets — 4' AMRAP:**

- 5 **Wall Walks**
- 5 **Lap Runs** (1 lap = 350 m)

**Even sets — 4' AMRAP:**

- 10 **Cal Machine**
- 10 m **Handstand Walk** or 30 m **Bear Walk**

**Clock**

- 0:00-4:00 wall walks + laps, then 1:00 rest
- 5:00-9:00 calories + handstand walk or bear walk, then 1:00 rest
- 10:00-14:00 wall walks + laps, then 1:00 rest
- 15:00-19:00 calories + handstand walk or bear walk, then 1:00 rest
- 20:00-24:00 wall walks + laps, then 1:00 rest
- 25:00-29:00 calories + handstand walk or bear walk, then 1:00 rest

**Target**: use the whole 1:00 rest. Odd sets — get the 5 wall walks in and run. Even sets — keep the couplet moving for all 4:00.

**Expected rounds, odd sets: 0 (5 wall walks + 1 lap; a full round is 5 laps / 1,750 m)**

**Expected rounds, even sets: 2-4 (one round is about 1:00-1:30)**

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: 3 wall walks; 30 m bear walk on the even sets
- **Scaling B**: shorter wall walk, feet to a line; 1 lap or 200 m; 8 cal + 20 m bear crawl

## Cooldown & Debrief

- 2:00 easy walk + breathing
- 30-45s wrist and lat stretch
- Debrief: shorter wall walks leave time for the lap
