# Friday (25/09/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: pull-up/wall space (wall walks + BMU/C2B), KBs 24/16, jump ropes, boxes 60/50, medballs 10/9
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; team intervals 30 min (6× 4:00 on / 1:00 off)
- **Stimulus**: partner I-go-you-go full rounds rotating three couplets under fixed work windows
- **Scaling A**: wall walk to line; KB 20/12; DU → 60 SU; BMU → C2B or jumping pull-ups; step-ups; lighter medball
- **Scaling B**: inchworms; Russian swings or DB snatches; 60 SU; ring rows; low box; sit-ups without ball
- **Logistics**: one wall/rig + KB + rope + box + ball per team; brief A/B/C rotation and full-round handoffs
- **Top Cues**:
  - **Wall walk**: “Hands back, lock elbows.”
  - **KB snatch**: “Snap hips, punch the bell up.”
  - **BMU / C2B**: “Hollow to arch, pull hard.”

## Warmup (15 min)

**Flow**

- **Before 0:00**: each team owns one lane — wall spot, rig spot, KB, 2 ropes, box, and ball, all within a few steps
- **Groups**: teams of 2 = workout teams; warm up in your own lane
- **Clock**: running clock; R2-R3 are paced by partner tags (tag = go), the same handoff as the workout

- **R1 RAISE (0:00-3:30, easy)**:
  - 0:00-2:00: team jog laps of the room, side by side
  - 2:00-3:30: in lane, swap every 20s — easy singles / inchworm walkouts
- **R2 ACTIVATE (3:30-9:00, moderate)**: tag-team, one partner works then tags; 3 rounds each:
  - 3 inchworms + 6 light KB deadlifts
  - 6 scap pulls + 6 kip swings
  - 6 med-ball sit-ups + 4 box step-ups
- **R3 POTENTIATE (9:00-13:00, hard)**: handoff rehearsal — one mini round of each couplet per partner, tag after each:
  - **A**: 1 wall walk (half height ok) + 4 light KB snatches
  - **B**: 15 DU / 30 SU + 2 BMU / C2B attempts
  - **C**: 4 box jumps + 4 ball sit-ups
- **Transition (13:00-15:00)**: set the KB to workout load; agree who starts window 1; coach recaps A → B → C and full-round handoffs

## Post-Warmup Mobility (60-90s)

- 30-45s wrist extension stretch
- 30-45s lat stretch each side

## Movement Prep

1 round of A (window 1) each at workout load — B and C were rehearsed in the warmup.

## Main Conditioning

### Team of 2 — 6 Rounds — 4:00 ON / 1:00 OFF

Rotate **A → B → C → A → B → C**. **I-go-you-go full round** (one athlete completes the couplet, then partner).

**A (rounds 1 & 4):**

- 3 **Wall Walks**
- 10 **KB Snatches** (24/16 kg)

**B (rounds 2 & 5):**

- 30 **Double-Unders**
- 3 **Bar Muscle-Ups** / 3 **Chest-to-Bar Pull-Ups**

**C (rounds 3 & 6):**

- 10 **Box Jumps** (60/50 cm)
- 10 **Sit-Ups with Ball** (10/9 kg)

**Target**: complete as many full partner rounds as possible inside each 4:00; rest the full 1:00. Log rounds per window.

**Expected**: ~3-6+ partner rounds per 4:00 window depending on station.

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: wall-walk to line, KB 20/12, 60 SU, C2B or jumping pull-ups, step-ups, lighter ball
- **Scaling B**: inchworms, DB snatches, 60 SU, ring rows, low box, unweighted sit-ups

## Cooldown & Debrief

- 2:00 easy walk
- 30-45s pec stretch each side
- Debrief: full-round handoffs beat splitting reps — keep the working athlete unbroken when possible
