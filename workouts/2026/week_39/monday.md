# Monday (21/09/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: rack + barbell + plates (back squat + hang squat cleans)
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; back squat ~15 min (E3MOM × 5); intervals 18 min (6× 2:00 on / 1:00 off)
- **Stimulus**: moderate-heavy squat volume, then alternating hang-clean buy-in / burpee-OTB filler intervals
- **Scaling A**: squat 80/50 or RPE 7; hang power cleans 30/20; step-over burpees
- **Scaling B**: goblet squats; DB hang cleans; down-ups
- **Logistics**: pair by rack for strength; strip to 40/25 for the intervals; brief odd/even a/b alternation
- **Top Cues**:
  - **Back squat**: “Drive the floor away.”
  - **Hang squat clean**: “Hips through, fast elbows, catch deep.”
  - **Burpee OTB**: “Chest down, two feet over.”

## Warmup (15 min)

**Flow**

- **Before 0:00**: racks at back-squat height; one PVC per athlete at the rack; tape a floor line beside each rack for burpees
- **Groups**: rack pairs = strength pairs; warm up at your own rack
- **Clock**: running clock; coach calls each block change

- **R1 RAISE (0:00-3:30, easy)**: lines of 3-4 across the floor, coach sends waves:
  - 2 lengths easy jog
  - 1 length high knees + 1 length butt kicks
  - 1 length lateral shuffle each way
  - 1 length walking lunge + reach
- **R2 ACTIVATE (3:30-9:00, moderate)**: at your rack, coach counts reps out loud and the whole group moves together; 3 rounds:
  - 8 air squats (3s down)
  - 6 PVC good mornings
  - 6 PVC hang muscle cleans
  - 4 down-ups
- **R3 POTENTIATE (9:00-13:00, hard)**: rack partners alternate (one works, one watches the catch); 3 rounds each:
  - 3 PVC hang squat cleans (fast elbows)
  - 3 jump squats
  - 3 burpees over the floor line
- **Transition (13:00-15:00)**: 5 empty-bar back squats each, then load the first build set

## Post-Warmup Mobility (60-90s)

- 30-45s ankle rocks each side
- 30-45s front rack stretch

## Strength / Skill

### A) Back Squat — Every 3:00 for 5 Sets

- 8 reps @ **100/60 kg**

**Target**: finish each set with ~1:00-1:30 to re-rack and reset before the next 3:00.

## Reset Window

2-3 min to set hang-clean load (40/25) and clear the floor for burpee OTB.

## Movement Prep

1 short a + 1 short b:

- 4 hang squat cleans @ workout load + 3 burpees over the bar
- 3 burpees over the bar + 4 hang squat cleans

## Main Conditioning

### B) 6 Sets — 2:00 ON / 1:00 OFF — Alternate a / b

**Odd sets (1, 3, 5) — a:**

- 10 **Hang Squat Cleans** (40/25 kg)
- **Max Burpees Over the Bar** for remaining time

**Even sets (2, 4, 6) — b:**

- 10 **Burpees Over the Bar**
- **Max Hang Squat Cleans** (40/25 kg) for remaining time

**Target**: finish the buy-in in ~0:40-1:00 so there are filler reps every interval. Log filler reps each set.

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: hang power cleans 30/20, step-over burpees
- **Scaling B**: DB hang cleans, down-ups

## Cooldown & Debrief

- 1:00 easy walk + breathing
- 30-45s couch stretch each side
- Quick debrief: protect the clean catch on odd sets so even-set max cleans still look identical
