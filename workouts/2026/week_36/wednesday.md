# Wednesday (02/09/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: jump ropes, mats, pull-up rig (BMU / pull-ups + dips), cones for 8 m shuttle
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; 25' AMRAP
- **Stimulus**: steady bodyweight AMRAP with rope, midline, and gymnastics density
- **Scaling A**: DU → 60 SU as written; elevate push-ups; BMU → pull-ups + dips; keep shuttle
- **Scaling B**: 60 SU always; knee push-ups; jumping pull-ups + box dips; reduce shuttle to 8
- **Logistics**: set 8 m shuttle cones; assign rig lanes; ropes staged at the station
- **Top Cues**:
  - **Double-under**: “Tall posture, quiet wrists.”
  - **Push-up**: “Chest to deck, lock out.”
  - **BMU / pull-up**: “Hollow to arch, pull hard.”

## Warmup (8-10 min)

- **R1 RAISE (2:00 easy)**: 2:00 easy jog or bike
- **R2 ACTIVATE (1:30 moderate)**: 2 rounds:
  - 20 single-unders
  - 8 sit-ups
  - 6 scap pulls + 6 kip swings
- **R3 POTENTIATE (1:00 hard)**: 2 rounds:
  - 10 double-under attempts (or 20 SU)
  - 6 push-ups
  - 2×8 m easy shuttle + 2 pull-ups / BMU practice

## Post-Warmup Mobility (60-90s)

- 30-45s wrist extension stretch
- 30-45s lat stretch each side

## Movement Prep

1 short rehearsal round:

- 15 DU / 30 SU
- 8 sit-ups
- 6 push-ups
- 2×8 m shuttle
- 2 BMU or 3 pull-ups + 3 dips

## Main Conditioning

### 25' AMRAP

- 30 **Double-Unders** / 60 **Single-Unders**
- 20 **Sit-Ups**
- 15 **Push-Ups**
- 10 **Shuttle Runs** (1 = 8 m)
- 5 **Bar Muscle-Ups** / 5 **Pull-Ups** + 5 **Dips**

**Expected rounds: 4-7 (steady early; push the last 5 min)**

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: 60 SU, elevate push-ups, pull-ups + dips for BMU, keep shuttle
- **Scaling B**: 60 SU, knee push-ups, jumping pull-ups + box dips, 8 shuttles

## Cooldown & Debrief

- 2:00 easy walk
- 30-45s forearm/grip shakeout
- Debrief: don’t redline the rope early — BMU / pull-up quality decides the round count

## Active Rest Alternative

Same shape, lower volume and no high-skill gymnastics.

### 20' AMRAP (easy)

- 40 **Single-Unders** (or 20 easy DU attempts)
- 12 **Sit-Ups**
- 10 elevated or knee **Push-Ups**
- 6 **Shuttle Walks / Easy Jogs** (1 = 8 m)
- 5 **Ring Rows** + 5 **Box Dips** (or bench dips)

**Expected rounds: 3-5 at conversational pace.** Stop early if form fades; no score chase.
