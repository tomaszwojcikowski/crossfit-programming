# Monday (31/08/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: rack + barbell + plates (back squat), KBs 24/16
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; back squat ~17.5 min (E2:30 × 7); interval piece 12 min (E2MOM × 6)
- **Stimulus**: heavy squat percentage wave, then alternating KB / burpee-squat intervals under a fixed clock
- **Scaling A**: squat by feel at RPE 7-8; KB 20/12; light back squats 30/20; step-over burpees
- **Scaling B**: goblet squat wave; Russian swings; DB squats; down-ups
- **Logistics**: pair by rack for strength; stage KB next to light bar before the reset; brief odd/even A-B alternation
- **Top Cues**:
  - **Back squat**: “Drive the floor away.”
  - **KB swing**: “Snap hips, float the bell.”
  - **Burpee OTB**: “Chest down, two feet over.”

## Warmup (8-10 min)

- **R1 RAISE (2:00 easy)**: 2:00 easy bike/row + nasal breathing
- **R2 ACTIVATE (1:30 moderate)**: 2 rounds:
  - 6 air squats
  - 6 KB deadlifts (light)
  - 4 down-ups
- **R3 POTENTIATE (1:00 hard)**: 2 rounds:
  - 4 jump squats
  - 6 KB swings (build toward workout load)
  - 3 burpees over the bar (empty)

## Post-Warmup Mobility (60-90s)

- 30-45s ankle rocks each side
- 30-45s hip opener each side

## Strength / Skill

### A) Back Squat — Every 2:30 for 7 Sets

Build through:

- 8×85%
- 7×85%
- 6×90%
- 5×90%
- 4×95%
- 3×95%
- 2×96% + 2 kg

**Target**: finish each set with ~45-75s to re-rack and reset before the next 2:30.

## Reset Window

2-3 min to strip the bar to 40/25, stage KB 24/16, and confirm A/B alternation.

## Movement Prep

1 short A + 1 short B:

- 6 KB swings + 6 goblet squats
- 3 burpees over the bar + 4 back squats @ 40/25

## Main Conditioning

### B) Every 2:00 × 6 Sets — Alternate A / B

**Odd sets (1, 3, 5) — A:**

- 10 **KB Swings** (24/16 kg)
- 15 **Goblet Squats** (24/16 kg)
- 10 **KB Swings** (24/16 kg)

**Even sets (2, 4, 6) — B:**

- 7 **Burpees Over the Bar**
- 14 **Back Squats** (40/25 kg)
- 7 **Burpees Over the Bar**

**Target**: finish each window in ~1:20-1:45 with rest before the next beep.

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: KB 20/12, back squats 30/20, step-over burpees
- **Scaling B**: Russian swings, DB squats, down-ups

## Cooldown & Debrief

- 1:00 easy walk + breathing
- 30-45s couch stretch each side
- Quick debrief: odd sets breathe through the goblets; even sets keep the squat unbroken

## Active Rest Alternative

For athletes who need to move without loading the heavy squat day or redlining the intervals.

**Strength (optional, light):** Every 2:30 × 5 — 5–6 empty-bar or goblet back/front squats @ easy RPE 5. Skip the % wave.

**Then: Every 2:00 × 6 — Alternate A / B (conversational)**

**Odd sets — A:**

- 8 **Russian KB Swings** (light)
- 10 **Goblet Squats** (light) or air squats
- 8 **Russian KB Swings**

**Even sets — B:**

- 5 **Down-Ups** (or step-over the bar)
- 10 **Air Squats** or empty-bar back squats
- 5 **Down-Ups**

**Target**: finish each window with ≥30s rest; nose breathing preferred. No score chase.
