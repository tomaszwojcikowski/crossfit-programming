# Tuesday (01/09/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: pull-up rig (T2B + HSPU), rower, air bike, ski/run lane, DBs or bands for curl / triceps
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; 30' EMOM (5 cycles of a 6-station rotation)
- **Stimulus**: long mixed EMOM — gymnastics, monostructural, and accessory work at sustainable density
- **Scaling A**: T2B → hanging knee raises; HSPU → pike/box HSPU; keep cal targets; lighter curl/ext loads
- **Scaling B**: V-ups; HR push-ups or DB strict press; reduce cals to 10/8; band curls / band pushdowns
- **Logistics**: assign stations in a clear rotation; brief which machine is “run/ski”; start everyone on minute 1 together
- **Top Cues**:
  - **T2B**: “Short sets, kick through.”
  - **HSPU**: “Kick up once, lock every rep.”
  - **Machines**: “Smooth pulls, leave rest.”

## Main Conditioning

### 30' EMOM

Rotate:

1. 12 **Toes-to-Bar**
2. 12/10 **Cal Row**
3. 12 **Biceps Curls** + 12 **Triceps Extensions**
4. 12/10 **Cal Air Bike**
5. 12 **Handstand Push-Ups**

**Target**: finish each minute with ~10-20s rest; scale immediately if you miss the window. Expect ~5 full cycles.

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: hanging knee raises, pike/box HSPU, keep cals, lighter arm work
- **Scaling B**: V-ups, HR push-ups / DB press, 10/8 cals, band curls / pushdowns
