# Thursday (03/09/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: barbell + plates (hang squat snatch 50/35), boxes 60/50, wall balls 10/6
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; team piece inside a 24-min window
- **Stimulus**: partner I-go-you-go snatch / box / wall-ball density, then burpee box-jump filler
- **Scaling A**: hang power snatch; step-ups; WB 9/6 or lower target; keep full-round handoffs
- **Scaling B**: DB hang snatch; low box step-ups; med-ball thrusters; reduce to 16 prescribed rounds if needed
- **Logistics**: one bar + box + wall ball per team; brief “full round then switch” and remaining-time filler
- **Top Cues**:
  - **Hang squat snatch**: “Hips back, catch deep.”
  - **Box jump**: “Soft land, step down.”
  - **Wall ball**: “Drive up, hit the target.”

- **R1 RAISE (2:00 easy)**: 2:00 easy bike/row
- **R2 ACTIVATE (1:30 moderate)**: 2 rounds:
  - 6 good mornings (empty bar / PVC)
  - 6 muscle snatches (empty bar / PVC)
  - 6 med-ball squats
- **R3 POTENTIATE (1:00 hard)**: 2 rounds:
  - 3 hang power snatches
  - 3 box jumps / step-ups
  - 4 wall balls
  - 2 burpee box jumps

## Post-Warmup Mobility (60-90s)

- 30-45s ankle rocks each side
- 30-45s overhead/lat stretch each side

## Movement Prep

Partner rehearsal (keep tiny):

- 1 full I-go-you-go round each: 2 hang squat snatches + 4 box jumps + 8 wall balls @ workout loads
- 2 burpee box jumps each

## Main Conditioning

### Team of 2 — In a 24' Window

**20 rounds I-go-you-go** (full round each before switch):

- 2 **Hang Squat Snatches** (50/35 kg)
- 4 **Box Jumps** (60/50 cm)
- 8 **Wall Ball Shots** (10/6 kg)

**Remaining time:** **Burpee Box Jumps** (split / I-go-you-go)

**Target**: finish the 20 prescribed rounds with time left for burpee box jumps; log filler reps.

**Expected**: most teams reach filler in the last 3-8 min; strong teams bank 20-60+ burpee box jumps.

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: hang power snatch, step-ups, lighter/lower WB, keep full-round handoffs
- **Scaling B**: DB hang snatch, low box, med-ball thrusters, 16 rounds then filler

## Cooldown & Debrief

- 2:00 easy walk + breathing
- 30-45s hamstring stretch each side
- Debrief: protect snatch quality early so the filler window opens

## Active Rest Alternative

Stay in the team format with light loads and no burpee filler redline.

### Team of 2 — 20' Window

**12–16 rounds I-go-you-go** (full round then switch):

- 2 **Hang Power Snatches** (PVC / empty bar / light DB)
- 4 **Box Step-Ups** (low box ok)
- 6 light **Wall Balls** or med-ball squats

**Remaining time:** easy walk + mobility, or slow step-ups (no burpee box jumps).

**Target**: smooth handoffs, nose breathing; stop when quality drops.
