# Monday (03/08/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: rack + barbell + plates (back squat + snatch)
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; back squat ~14 min (E2MOM × 7); snatch complex 15 min (E1MOM × 15)
- **Stimulus**: percentage squat wave for strength, then technical snatch complex building load under a fixed clock
- **Scaling A**: squat by feel at RPE 7-8; hang power snatch + hang power snatch if full squat snatch is inconsistent
- **Scaling B**: goblet squat wave; DB snatch + hang DB snatch (build load)
- **Logistics**: pair by rack for squats; clear floor space for snatch complexes; brief build-not-miss intent before the EMOM
- **Top Cues**:
  - **Back squat**: “Drive the floor away.”
  - **Squat snatch**: “Pull under, catch deep.”
  - **Hang squat snatch**: “Hips back, bar close, catch fast.”

## Warmup (8-10 min)

- **R1 RAISE (2:00 easy)**: 2:00 easy bike/row + nasal breathing
- **R2 ACTIVATE (1:30 moderate)**: 2 rounds:
  - 6 air squats
  - 6 good mornings (empty bar or PVC)
  - 6 muscle snatches (empty bar / PVC)
- **R3 POTENTIATE (1:00 hard)**: 2 rounds:
  - 4 jump squats
  - 3 hang power snatches (empty bar)
  - 2 overhead squats (empty bar)

## Post-Warmup Mobility (60-90s)

- 30-45s ankle rocks each side
- 30-45s overhead/lat stretch each side

## Strength / Skill

### A) Back Squat — Every 2:00 for 7 Sets

Build through:

- 8×72%
- 7×75%
- 6×77%
- 5×80%
- 3×85%
- 3×85%
- 2×87%

**Target**: finish each set with ~30-60s to re-rack and reset before the next 2:00.

## Reset Window

2-3 min to clear squat plates, strip to snatch starting load, and claim floor lanes.

## Movement Prep

1-2 crisp complexes, empty → light:

- 1 squat snatch + 1 hang squat snatch
- Build only if positions look identical

## Main Conditioning

### B) Every 1:00 × 15 Sets

- 1 **Squat Snatch** + 1 **Hang Squat Snatch**

Build weight across the 15 minutes. Misses count as a set — drop load and keep moving.

**Target**: finish each complex in ~15-25s with quality positions; build only when both catches are solid.

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: hang power snatch + hang power snatch, or power snatch + hang squat snatch
- **Scaling B**: DB snatch + hang DB snatch (alternate arms each minute if needed)

## Cooldown & Debrief

- 1:00 easy walk + breathing
- 30-45s couch stretch each side
- Quick debrief: squat depth first; snatch builds only when the catch is quiet
