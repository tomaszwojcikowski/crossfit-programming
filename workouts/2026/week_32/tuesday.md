# Tuesday (04/08/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: pull-up rig (wall walks + pull-ups), KBs 24/16, jump ropes
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; interval piece 24 min (E4MOM × 6)
- **Stimulus**: mixed-modal intervals that finish inside each 4:00 with wall-walk skill under fatigue
- **Scaling A**: wall walks → 3 walk-ins to a clear marker or 6-8 inchworms; banded pull-ups; KB 20/12; DU → 52 SU
- **Scaling B**: 6 plank-to-down-dog; ring rows; Russian KB swings; 52 SU always; elevate push-ups
- **Logistics**: assign wall + rig lanes; stage KB and rope at the station; brief “finish with rest” so sets don’t bleed into the next beep
- **Top Cues**:
  - **Wall walk**: “Walk hands back, lock elbows.”
  - **Pull-up**: “Chest to bar path, short sets.”
  - **KB snatch**: “Snap hips, punch the bell up.”

  - 1-2 wall walk practice (or scale)
  - 4 jumping pull-ups / kipping practice

- 30-45s lat stretch each side

1 short rehearsal set (~2:00):

- 1 wall walk (or scale)
- 5 pull-ups
- 5 KB snatches (workout load)
- 5 push-ups
- 10 double-under attempts (or 20 SU)

## Main Conditioning

- 3 **Wall Walks**
- 13 **Pull-Ups**
- 13 **KB Snatches** (24/16 kg)
- 13 **Push-Ups**
- 26 **Double-Unders**

**Target**: finish each round in ~2:30-3:30 with 30-90s rest before the next interval.

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: wall-walk to marker / inchworms, banded pull-ups, KB 20/12, 52 SU if DU fail under fatigue
- **Scaling B**: plank-to-down-dog, ring rows, Russian swings, elevated push-ups, 52 SU
