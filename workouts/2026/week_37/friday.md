# Friday (11/09/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: pull-up rig (pull-ups + T2B), barbell + plates (deadlift 100/65, clean & jerk 80/45)
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; team 25' AMRAP
- **Stimulus**: partner I-go-you-go barbell + gymnastics blocks with a down-up buy-through each AMRAP round
- **Scaling A**: banded pull-ups; DL 80/50; T2B → hanging knee raises; C&J 60/35; keep IGYG by exercise
- **Scaling B**: ring rows; DB deadlifts; V-ups; DB clean & jerk / hang power clean + push press; reduce down-ups to 30
- **Logistics**: one rig lane + two bar loads (or one bar with plate changes) per team; brief “by exercise” handoffs
- **Top Cues**:
  - **Deadlift**: “Push the floor away.”
  - **Clean & jerk**: “Jump, then punch.”
  - **T2B**: “Short sets, kick through.”

## Warmup (8-10 min)

- **R1 RAISE (2:00 easy)**: 2:00 easy bike/row
- **R2 ACTIVATE (1:30 moderate)**: 2 rounds:
  - 6 down-ups
  - 6 empty-bar deadlifts
  - 6 kip swings
- **R3 POTENTIATE (1:00 hard)**: 2 rounds:
  - 3 pull-ups
  - 3 hang power cleans + 2 push jerks
  - 3 T2B / hanging knee raises

## Post-Warmup Mobility (60-90s)

- 30-45s hamstring stretch each side
- 30-45s front rack stretch

## Movement Prep

Partner rehearsal (keep tiny):

- 4 pull-ups each (IGYG by exercise)
- 4 deadlifts @ workout load each
- 4 T2B each
- 2 clean & jerks @ workout load each
- 4 down-ups

## Main Conditioning

### Team of 2 — 25' AMRAP

Each AMRAP round:

**2 rounds I-go-you-go by exercise:**

- 10 **Pull-Ups**
- 10 **Deadlifts** (100/65 kg)

**Then 2 rounds I-go-you-go by exercise:**

- 10 **Toes-to-Bar**
- 5 **Clean & Jerks** (80/45 kg)

**Then:**

- 50 **Down-Ups** (split)

**I-go-you-go by exercise**: one athlete completes the movement, then the partner completes the same movement before moving on.

**Expected rounds: 1-3 (steady handoffs; don’t redline the first deadlift block)**

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: banded pull-ups, DL 80/50, hanging knee raises, C&J 60/35
- **Scaling B**: ring rows, DB deadlifts, V-ups, DB C&J, 30 down-ups

## Cooldown & Debrief

- 2:00 easy walk + breathing
- 30-45s forearm/grip shakeout
- Debrief: treat the 50 down-ups as a reset — early barbell quality funds later rounds
