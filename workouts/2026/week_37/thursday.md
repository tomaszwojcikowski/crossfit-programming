# Thursday (10/09/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: KBs/DBs (lunges, Bulgarian split squats, farmer), bench or floor for press, DBs for triceps, heavy implement for hip thrust
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; accessory EMOM-style 22.5 min (E75s × 18)
- **Stimulus**: bodybuilding-style density — 3 full cycles through a 6-station rotation under a fixed clock
- **Scaling A**: lighter loads; reduce to 12-14 reps; shorter farmer carry if needed
- **Scaling B**: bodyweight lunges / split squats; band press / band triceps; glute bridge; suitcase carry light
- **Logistics**: pre-stage 6 stations; athletes rotate in order; start together on station 1
- **Top Cues**:
  - **Lunges / split squat**: “Long step, knee tracks.”
  - **Press**: “Ribs down, lock out.”
  - **Farmer carry**: “Tall posture, short steps.”

## Warmup (8-10 min)

- **R1 RAISE (2:00 easy)**: 2:00 easy bike/row
- **R2 ACTIVATE (1:30 moderate)**: 2 rounds:
  - 6 alternating reverse lunges
  - 6 push-ups
  - 6 hip bridges
- **R3 POTENTIATE (1:00 hard)**: 2 rounds:
  - 4 curtsey lunges each side (light)
  - 4 floor/bench press (light)
  - 20 m easy farmer carry

## Post-Warmup Mobility (60-90s)

- 30-45s hip opener each side
- 30-45s pec stretch each side

## Movement Prep

Quick pass through each station (tiny reps):

- 4 curtsey lunges / side
- 4 bench or floor press
- 3 Bulgarian split squats / side
- 4 triceps extensions
- 4 hip thrusts
- 20 m farmer carry

## Main Conditioning

### Every 75s × 18 Sets

Rotate through (3 full cycles):

1. 16-20 **Curtsey Lunges** with KB/DB (total reps)
2. 16-20 **Bench Press** or **Floor Press**
3. 16-20 **Bulgarian Split Squats** with KB (total reps)
4. 16-20 **Triceps Extensions** with DB
5. 16-20 **Hip Thrusts** (heavy)
6. 45s **Farmer Carry** (2× heavy KB)

**Target**: finish work with ~10-20s before the next beep; choose a load that stays in the 16-20 window without grinding.

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: lighter loads, 12-14 reps, shorter carry
- **Scaling B**: bodyweight lunges/split squats, band press/triceps, glute bridge, light suitcase carry

## Cooldown & Debrief

- 2:00 easy walk
- 30-45s hip flexor stretch each side
- Debrief: leave a rep or two in the tank — quality across all 18 beeps beats early ego loads
