# Tuesday (08/09/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: wall space (HSW / wall walks / HSPU), pull-up rig (BMU)
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; HSW practice 10 min; interval piece 20 min (E5MOM × 4)
- **Stimulus**: handstand skill volume, then gymnastics couplet density inside fixed 5:00 windows
- **Scaling A**: HSW → wall walk or bear crawl; HSPU → pike/box HSPU; BMU → C2B or jumping BMU
- **Scaling B**: inchworm to wall; HR push-ups or DB strict press; pull-ups or ring rows
- **Logistics**: assign wall + rig lanes; mark 12 m HSW lines; brief “finish inside 5:00” before the intervals
- **Top Cues**:
  - **Handstand walk**: “Tall stack, short steps.”
  - **HSPU**: “Kick up once, lock every rep.”
  - **BMU**: “Hollow to arch, pull hard.”

- **R1 RAISE (2:00 easy)**: 2:00 easy bike/row
- **R2 ACTIVATE (1:30 moderate)**: 2 rounds:
  - 4 inchworms
  - 6 scap pulls + 6 kip swings
  - 6 hollow rocks
- **R3 POTENTIATE (1:00 hard)**: 2 rounds:
  - 1 wall walk / HSW practice
  - 2 pike push-ups / HSPU
  - 2 pull-ups / BMU attempts

## Post-Warmup Mobility (60-90s)

- 30-45s wrist extension stretch
- 30-45s lat stretch each side

## Strength / Skill

### A) Practice Handstand Walk

Build entries, balance, and short walks. Scale to wall walks or bear crawls as needed.

**Time cap: 10 min**

## Reset Window

1-2 min to claim wall + rig for the interval piece.

## Movement Prep

1 short rehearsal:

- 6-8 m HSW or 1 wall walk
- 3 HSPU or scale
- 2 BMU or scale

## Main Conditioning

### B) Every 5:00 × 4 Sets

- 12 m **Handstand Walk** / **Wall Walk**
- 9 **Handstand Push-Ups**
- 6 **Bar Muscle-Ups**
- 9 **Handstand Push-Ups**
- 12 m **Handstand Walk** / **Wall Walk**

**Target**: finish each set in ~3:00-4:30 with rest before the next beep.

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: wall walk or bear crawl for HSW, pike/box HSPU, C2B or jumping BMU
- **Scaling B**: inchworms, HR push-ups / DB press, pull-ups or ring rows; reduce HSPU to 6+6 if needed

- 30-45s pec stretch each side
