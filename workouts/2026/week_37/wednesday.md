# Wednesday (09/09/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: air bikes, rowers, wall balls 10/6, mats for push-ups / sit-ups
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; team-of-3 for-time chipper (budget ~30-35 min work)
- **Stimulus**: long shared monostructural chipper with wall-ball tax on every machine switch
- **Scaling A**: elevate push-ups; sit-ups without ball or lighter ball; keep switch distances
- **Scaling B**: knee push-ups; unweighted sit-ups; reduce totals ~25% if needed; shorter switch tax (10 WB)
- **Logistics**: one bike + one rower + one wall ball per team of 3; whiteboard the switch rule before the clock
- **Top Cues**:
  - **Bike / row**: “Smooth pulls, short switches.”
  - **Wall ball**: “Drive up, hit the target.”
  - **Push-up**: “Chest to deck, lock out.”

## Warmup (8-10 min)

- **R1 RAISE (2:00 easy)**: 2:00 easy bike or row
- **R2 ACTIVATE (1:30 moderate)**: 2 rounds:
  - 6 down-ups
  - 8 sit-ups
  - 6 med-ball squats
- **R3 POTENTIATE (1:00 hard)**: 2 rounds:
  - 8 hard bike cals
  - 6 push-ups
  - 6 wall balls
  - 8 hard row cals

## Post-Warmup Mobility (60-90s)

- 30-45s hip opener each side
- 30-45s wrist extension stretch

## Movement Prep

Team rehearsal (keep tiny):

- 100-200 m easy bike with one practice switch + 5 wall balls
- 4 push-ups each
- 100 m easy row with one practice switch + 5 wall balls
- 4 sit-ups with ball

## Main Conditioning

### Team of 3 — For Time

- 6000 m **Air Bike**
- 150 **Push-Ups**
- 2250 m **Row**
- 150 **Sit-Ups with Ball** (10/6 kg)

**Switch rules**

- Air bike: change every **500 m**
- Row: change every **250 m**
- **Every change = 15 Wall Ball Shots** (10/6 kg) for the team before the next athlete continues

**Time cap: 35 min (expected: 22-32 min)**

Push-ups and sit-ups: split any way. Machines follow the switch distances above.

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: elevate push-ups, lighter/no-ball sit-ups, keep switch tax
- **Scaling B**: knee push-ups, unweighted sit-ups, 10 WB per change, or ~25% less total volume

## Cooldown & Debrief

- 2:00 easy walk
- 30-45s hamstring stretch each side
- Debrief: treat wall balls as the tax for greedy long pulls — short switches usually win
