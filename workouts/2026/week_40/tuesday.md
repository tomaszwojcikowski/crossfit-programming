## Warmup (15 min)

**Flow**

- **Before 0:00**: DL bars staged; wall lanes marked; empty bars / PVC for presses
- **Groups**: DL partners share a platform; same partners share a wall for HSPU
- **Clock**: running clock; coach calls block changes

- **R1 RAISE (0:00-3:30, easy)**: lines across the floor, coach sends waves:
  - 2 lengths easy jog
  - 1 length bear crawl
  - 1 length inchworm to push-up
  - 1 length skip with arm circles
- **R2 ACTIVATE (3:30-9:00, moderate)**: partner A on bar / partner B on wall, swap each round; 3 rounds each:
  - **Bar**: 6 PVC / empty-bar Romanian deadlifts + 4 hip hinges with pause
  - **Wall**: 20s handstand hold or pike hold + 4 elevated push-ups
- **R3 POTENTIATE (9:00-13:00, hard)**: same swap, faster; 3 rounds each:
  - **Bar**: 3 light deadlifts (build toward strength load feel)
  - **Wall**: 2-3 strict HSPU / pike HSPU attempts
- **Transition (13:00-15:00)**: load first DL set (120/80 or scaled); confirm odd/even station order
