# Friday (02/10/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: barbell + plates (bear complex + thrusters 50/35), calorie machines, lap run lane
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; team 26' AMRAP
- **Stimulus**: long partner chipper-style AMRAP with heavyish barbell cycling and a shared monostructural closer each round
- **Scaling A**: complex/thrusters 40/25; step-over burpees; keep cal or lap option
- **Scaling B**: DB bear complex + DB thrusters; down-ups; reduce burpees to 30; half-lap if needed
- **Logistics**: one bar + machine per team; brief bear-complex standard and who splits what; runs are **together**
- **Top Cues**:
  - **Bear complex**: “Quiet catch, punch overhead, re-rack smooth.”
  - **Burpee OTB**: “Chest down, two feet over.”
  - **Thruster**: “Hips through, punch the ceiling.”

## Warmup (15 min)

**Flow**

- **Before 0:00**: one empty bar + PVC per team lane; machines at the back; lap route confirmed
- **Groups**: teams of 2 = workout teams; warm up in your lane
- **Clock**: running clock; R2-R3 use partner tags (same handoff idea as the AMRAP)

- **R1 RAISE (0:00-3:30, easy)**:
  - 0:00-2:00: team jog laps side by side (include short lap feel)
  - 2:00-3:30: in lane, swap every 20s — easy singles / down-ups
- **R2 ACTIVATE (3:30-9:00, moderate)**: tag-team; 3 rounds each:
  - 4 PVC / empty-bar hang muscle cleans + 4 front squats
  - 4 push presses + 4 back-rack squats (PVC / empty)
  - 4 down-ups over the bar line
- **R3 POTENTIATE (9:00-13:00, hard)**: tag after each mini block; 2 rounds each:
  - 1 empty-bar bear complex (PC + FS + PP + BS + PP)
  - 3 burpees over the bar
  - 3 empty-bar thrusters
  - 5 hard machine cals (split) or 50 m easy jog together
- **Transition (13:00-15:00)**: load bar to 50/35 (or scaled); agree who starts the complex; coach recaps complex standard + cal vs lap choice

## Post-Warmup Mobility (60-90s)

- 30-45s hip opener each side

## Main Conditioning

### Team of 2 — 26' AMRAP

Each round:

- 20 **Bear Complexes** (50/35 kg) (split)
- 40 **Burpees Over the Bar** (split)
- 20 **Thrusters** (50/35 kg) (split)
- 40 **Cal Machine** (split) **/ ** `1 Lap Run` **together** (team chooses one per round)

**Bear complex (1 rep)** = 1 Power Clean + 1 Front Squat + 1 Push Press + 1 Back Squat + 1 Push Press

**Expected rounds: 1-3 (steady early; push the last 5 min)**

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: 40/25, step-over burpees, keep cal or lap
- **Scaling B**: DB complex + DB thrusters, 30 burpees / down-ups, half-lap option

- 30-45s pec stretch each side
