# Thursday (01/10/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: pull-up rig (T2B + BMU), barbell or DBs (strict press), medballs, jump ropes
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; 25' EMOM (5 cycles of a 5-station rotation)
- **Stimulus**: long mixed EMOM — gymnastics density, pressing, midline hold, and rope rhythm
- **Scaling A**: T2B → hanging knee raises; BMU → C2B or jumping pull-ups; lighter press; DU → 60 SU
- **Scaling B**: V-ups; ring rows; DB push press; shorter hold; 60 SU always
- **Logistics**: assign stations in a clear rotation; stage medballs and ropes at the bay; start everyone on minute 1 together
- **Top Cues**:
  - **T2B**: “Short sets, kick through.”
  - **BMU**: “Hollow to arch, pull hard.”
  - **Bear-hug hold**: “Ribs down, squeeze the ball.”

## Warmup (15 min)

**Flow**

- **Before 0:00**: bands on rig; medballs and ropes staged in a ring around the bay; empty bars / DBs for press
- **Groups**: number off into lanes of 3-4 that will share a rig bay for the EMOM
- **Clock**: running clock; coach calls “Rotate” for station changes

- **R1 RAISE (0:00-3:30, easy)**: lines, coach sends waves:
  - 2 lengths easy jog
  - 1 length skip
  - 20s easy single-unders (in place)
  - 1 length bear crawl
- **R2 ACTIVATE (3:30-9:00, moderate)**: 5-station lane pass, ~45s each, light; one full pass:
  - **Rig**: 5 scap pulls + 5 kip swings
  - **Press**: 6 empty-bar / light DB strict press
  - **Pull**: 3 jumping pull-ups or ring rows
  - **Midline**: 20s medball bear-hug hold (light ball)
  - **Rope**: 30s easy singles
- **R3 POTENTIATE (9:00-13:00, hard)**: same 5 stations, ~30s each, faster; one full pass:
  - 4 hanging knee raises / T2B
  - 5 strict press (light → workout feel)
  - 2 BMU / C2B attempts
  - 20s harder bear-hug hold
  - 15s fast singles / DU attempts
- **Transition (13:00-15:00)**: set workout press load; confirm minute order 1→5 and who starts where if sharing

## Post-Warmup Mobility (60-90s)

- 30-45s lat stretch each side
- 30-45s wrist extension stretch

## Movement Prep

Tiny rehearsal of minutes 1-3 only (already covered in warmup):

- 4 T2B
- 4 strict press @ workout load
- 1-2 BMU / C2B

## Main Conditioning

### 25' EMOM

Rotate:

1. 12 **Toes-to-Bar**
2. 12 **Strict Press**
3. 6 **Bar Muscle-Ups**
4. 45s **Medball Bear-Hug Hold**
5. 30 **Double-Unders**

**Target**: finish each minute with ~10-20s rest; scale immediately if you miss the window. Expect 5 full cycles.

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: hanging knee raises, lighter press, C2B or jumping pull-ups, 60 SU
- **Scaling B**: V-ups, DB push press, ring rows, shorter hold, 60 SU always

## Cooldown & Debrief

- 2:00 easy walk
- 30-45s forearm/grip shakeout
- Debrief: protect BMU minutes early — the hold and rope should still feel available in cycle 5
