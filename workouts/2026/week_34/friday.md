# Friday (21/08/2026)

**Setup**

- **Equipment**: DBs 22.5/15, boxes for step-overs
- **Timing**: 60-min class cap; team for-time 26-min cap
- **Stimulus**: partner I-go-you-go DB triplets with a down-up middle buy-through
- **Scaling A**: DB 15/10; step-overs without load if needed; elevate push-ups; keep full-round handoffs
- **Scaling B**: lighter single DB; reduce to 2/4/6 and 6/4/2; 40 down-ups
- **Logistics**: one DB pair + box per team; brief “full round then switch” before the clock
- **Top Cues**:
  - **Devil press**: “Hips through, bells lock out.”
  - **Box step-over**: “Both DBs stay quiet, keep turning over.”
  - **Push press**: “Dip straight, punch up.”

- **R3 POTENTIATE (1:00 hard)**: 2 rounds:
  - 2 devil presses (light)
  - 4 box step-overs with DBs
  - 4 DB push press

- 1 full IGYG round each of Block 2 (8+6+4) @ workout load

## Main Conditioning

### Team of 2 — For Time

**Block 1 — 10 rounds I-go-you-go** (full round each before switch):

- 3 **Devil Presses** (2×22.5/15 kg)
- 6 **Box Step-Overs** with 2 DBs
- 9 **Push-Ups**

**Then:** 50 **Down-Ups** (split)

**Block 2 — 10 rounds I-go-you-go** (full round each before switch):

- 8 **Push Press** (1 DB)
- 6 **Box Step-Overs** with 1 DB

**Time cap: 26 min (expected: 18-24 min)**

**I-go-you-go standard**: one athlete completes the full triplet before the partner starts.

**Scaling options**

- **Scaling A**: DB 15/10, unloaded step-overs ok, elevate push-ups, keep full-round handoffs
- **Scaling B**: lighter DB, 2/4/6 and 6/4/2 triplets, 40 down-ups
